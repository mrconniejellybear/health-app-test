USER PRIVACY NOTICE

Data entered into this application are stored locally on your device via JavaScript LocalStorage. When data are displayed, they are retrived from your device's local stoarge via JSON.parse—a function that encodes complex data strings, such as dates and variables. Unlike our predatory competitors, we do not store user data on backend servers. This means we DO NOT and CANNOT view, control, store, or sell your entries for any category. It is inacessible to anyone without this device physically in their hand.

Should you wish to obtain records of your own data for personal/medical reasons, I am working on making exportable xmls files that will only be accessible to a single user. That's not ready yet, though.

Given that many companies are also big fat liars, the source code, written by Connor Mausolf, is available via Github, should you wish to inspect it.

One of this app's main objectives is to offer genuine privacy to anyone using it. Complete transparency. No creepy shit.
